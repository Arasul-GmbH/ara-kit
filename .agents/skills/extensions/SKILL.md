Procedure: `.ara/knowledge/extensions.md`

Short:

- **Written at the partner, built on the device.** The package goes there as an archive,
  the device builds the container. Development never happens against customer data on your
  own laptop.
- Understand the work step first, not the wished-for solution. What decides is what happens
  when the result is wrong once.
- Before building, look at what the product can already do. The device's contract says that,
  `node .ara/tools/app.mjs --device <device> --contract`, not your memory. Most wishes need
  a setup, not development.
- Start small: one example that runs through and that the customer has seen.
- Handing over means: using it, recognising that it runs, knowing what to do when it does not.
- A service, offered and billed separately.
