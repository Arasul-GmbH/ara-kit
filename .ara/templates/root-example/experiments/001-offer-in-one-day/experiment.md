# 001 A third person calculates a mould from the table

Status: planned
Assumption: A1, see `../../company/assumptions.md`
Created: {{day-8}} · Due: {{day+60}}

## Thesis

With the table of the last ten moulds an employee who never calculated gets within ten
percent of the price the partners would have named.

## What the mistake costs, what the test costs

If the thesis is wrong, the north goal is not reachable this way and the partners stay
the bottleneck. The test costs one afternoon of an employee and one hour of each partner.

## The test

One finished mould the employee does not know. They get the drawing and the table and
calculate alone. The real calculation stays in the drawer until they are done.

## Criterion

| Nr | What | Confirmed | Refuted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F1 | Difference between the blind price and the real one | ten percent or less | over 25 percent |
| F2 | Questions to the partners during the calculation | two or fewer | the employee gives up |

In between the assumption stays open and the test is repeated with a second mould.
