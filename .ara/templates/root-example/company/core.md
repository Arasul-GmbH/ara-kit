## What the house does

Hafenkante Toolmaking builds injection moulds for makers of pumps and fittings. 14
people, one hall, two managing partners who decide together. A mould is offered,
designed, built, sampled and handed over, and most of the money is lost in the first
step and in the last.

## Situation

The order book is full for the quarter. Offers take five working days on average,
because the calculation lives in one head. The shop floor books machine hours in the
app since the summer, the office still copies them into the calculation by hand.

## Bottleneck

The offer: as long as it takes a week, the fast customers go somewhere else.

## What is expressly not done

- No second product next to moulds, also no contract manufacturing to fill the hall
- No own website work, the agency does that
- No tool that needs an account per employee with a foreign provider
