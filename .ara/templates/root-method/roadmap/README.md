# Roadmap

One sheet per place: what it has to be able to do by when. *How* it is built the place
decides. The sheet of a place is called like the place in `../.claude/places.json`,
`root.md` holds the goals of this root itself.

Every goal is a line with a number, a milestone out of `../company/goal.md` and a
deadline. A goal without a milestone is an idea and belongs on a card under `backlog/`.
When the deadline lies within 14 days, the goal stands in `../company/follow-ups.md`
with its number.

```
| Nr | Goal | Milestone | Deadline | State |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| K1 | what is observable when it is reached | M1 | YYYY-MM-DD | open |
```

The letter in front of the number belongs to the place, one letter per place.
